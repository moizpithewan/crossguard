Crossguard is a pedestrian safety app that alerts children before they walk into danger zones on the street. These zones are set by a parent by entering a PIN into the app to enter "parent mode." The parent can then add restricted zones onto a map by tapping on the map, or selecting their current location, and setting a radius and name. When the child walks within 50 meters of a zone, the app sends a notification to the child warning them that they are approaching that zone. Once they enter, they get another notification, as well as an audio warning and a vibration. Location monitoring keeps running in the background, and all zones and settings are stored on the phone.
